How it works

Scan, order, split. That's the whole night.

No app and no account. Scanning opens the menu straight in your browser.

Scan the table code

Order together

Friends join your table with a 4-digit code and order from their own phones. Your order goes straight to the kitchen and bar.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Scan the table card and the menu opens in your browser. Signing in is optional; it keeps your past visits and any offers in one place.

How do I pay?

Your share shows on your phone. Pay your waiter by cash, card or mobile money, as you normally would. Paying inside Wasabi is coming later.

What do we need to buy?

Nothing special. Your team uses phones or tablets you already have, and you print the table cards from Wasabi on any printer.

Does it replace our waiters?

No. Waiters still welcome guests, serve and take payment. They just stop running back and forth with orders, so each one can look after more tables.

How long does setup take?

Most places are ready in an afternoon: add your menu, set your tables, print the cards and give each waiter a PIN. We help with all of it.

See it on your own tables.

We can come to your bar, café or restaurant and run a live demo on a few phones, so you and your team can try it before you decide.
