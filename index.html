How it works

Scan, order, split. That's the whole night.

No app to install and no account needed. The phone camera opens your menu in the browser.

Scan the table code

Each table has its own QR card. Diners see your menu with photos, prices and what's sold out tonight.

Everyone orders

Friends join the table with a 4-digit code and order from their own phones. Drinks go to the bar, food to the kitchen.

Split the bill

Split equally or by what each person ordered. Diners see their share, call the waiter and pay as they do today.

For your restaurant

Built for a busy Friday night.

Everything your team needs runs on ordinary phones and tablets. No tills or special hardware to buy.

Kitchen, bar and grill screens

Each station sees only its own orders, marks them preparing and ready, and the waiter knows when to pick up.

Waiters on their own phones

Waiters take tables and get that table's "Call waiter" and "Bill please" alerts. Diners see when help is on the way.

A live dashboard

Sales by hour and by item, how long orders take, and how each waiter and station is doing.

Change the menu in seconds

Edit prices, add specials or mark something sold out, and every table sees it straight away. No reprinting.

Your look, not ours

Your logo, colours and fonts on every screen diners see. Wasabi stays in the background.

Safe from pranks

Every table code carries a secret key, so nobody can order from outside. Waiters start each shift with a PIN.

Questions

Good to know.

Do diners need to download an app?

No. They point their phone camera at the table card and your menu opens in the browser. Signing in is optional; it lets regulars see past visits and offers.

What do we need to buy?

Nothing special. Your team uses phones or tablets you already have, and you print the table cards from Wasabi on any printer.

Does it replace our waiters?

No. Waiters still welcome guests, serve and take payment. They just stop running back and forth with orders, so each one can look after more tables.

Can diners pay inside the app?

Not yet. Today diners see their share and pay your waiter. Payment in the app is on our roadmap.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants are ready in an afternoon: add your menu, set your tables, print the cards and give each waiter a PIN. We help with all of it.
