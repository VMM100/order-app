Getting your menu into Wasabi

Send your menu any way that's easy for you. The quickest is our spreadsheet: it loads in one go.

1. Wasabi spreadsheet
Owner page → Menu → Download template. Fill it in with Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers, then upload it on the same page.
2. Your own file
An Excel sheet, Google Sheet or PDF of your menu. We'll copy it into the Wasabi spreadsheet for you.
3. Photos
Clear photos of each menu page, sent on WhatsApp to . We'll type it in.

The spreadsheet, column by column

SectionThe heading the item sits under on the menu: Drinks, Starters, Mains… Goes toWhere the order is made: Bar or Kitchen (or another station you've set up, like Grill). ItemThe name diners see. OptionA size or choice with its own price. One row per price: a beer in two sizes is two rows with the same Item. Price (TSh)Numbers only, no commas or "TSh": 9000, not TSh 9,000. DescriptionOne short line diners see under the name (optional). TagA small label such as Vegetarian, Spicy or ABV 4.5% (optional). Sold outyes or no. You can also change this any night from the bar or kitchen screen.

Tips

  • Allergy information belongs in the Description (for example "Contains nuts"). You are responsible for it being right.
  • Photos of dishes are optional; add them later from the owner page, one item at a time.
  • Prices are what diners see and what the bill adds up, so check them carefully before the first night.
