Quick start

Waiters

Stuck? Call or WhatsApp Wasabi
  1. Open the waiter link from your manager, tap your name and enter your PIN. Tap Turn on so your phone rings even when the screen is off.
  2. A new table appears: tap Accept. The table now sees your name.
  3. Your phone rings for Call waiter or Bill please: tap On my way so the table knows.
  4. Food or drinks show Ready: take them out, then tap Served.
  5. Tap Open bill to see each person's share, take payment as usual, then Bill delivered.
  6. Going on break? Hand over your tables. Finished? End shift.

Never share your PIN. Bills in Wasabi are not EFD receipts: issue the fiscal receipt as usual.

Quick start

Bar

Stuck? Call or WhatsApp Wasabi
  1. The bar screen stays open on the bar tablet, with the sound on. New drink orders appear on it and print on the bar printer.
  2. Tap Start when you begin an order and Ready when it's done (or All ready). The waiter is told.
  3. Run out of something? Mark it Sold out: diners can't order it until you switch it back.
  4. Ticket didn't print? Tap Reprint. A printer shown in red is down: tickets print once it's back.
  5. Walk-up customers: Start tab, Add round, then Close tab.

Printer needs a break (paper, cleaning)? Switch it off on this screen with a reason. Switching it back on asks whether to print what came in meanwhile.

Quick start

Kitchen

Stuck? Call or WhatsApp Wasabi
  1. The kitchen screen stays open on the kitchen tablet, with the sound on. New food orders appear on it and print on the kitchen printer.
  2. Tap Start when you begin cooking and Ready when the plate is up. The waiter is told.
  3. Run out of something? Mark it Sold out so nobody can order it.
  4. Ticket didn't print? Tap Reprint. If the printer shows red, carry on from the screen: missed tickets print when it's back.
  5. Read the note on each order: allergies and "no onions" are written there.

Screen or internet down? Use the paper order pad and tell the manager.
