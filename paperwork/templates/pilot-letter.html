1The pilot

The restaurant uses Wasabi free of charge from until (the pilot). Diners order at the table by scanning a QR code, waiters, kitchen and bar see orders on their screens, and the table's bill can be split. Diners keep paying the restaurant as usual; Wasabi does not handle payments.

2What Wasabi does

  • Sets up the menu, tables, QR table cards, staff codes and waiters' PINs, and trains the staff.
  • Sets up a print box so order tickets also print on the restaurant's own bar and kitchen printers, alongside the Wasabi screens.
  • Is on call during opening hours on , and fixes problems as quickly as it can.
  • Looks after the data as set out in section 4.

3What the restaurant does

  • Keeps a simple way to take orders without Wasabi (for example, a paper pad) in case of a power, network or service problem.
  • Is responsible for its menu, prices and allergy information, and keeps issuing fiscal (EFD) receipts as the law requires. Wasabi's bills are not fiscal receipts.
  • Keeps table cards on the tables, and staff codes and PINs private; tells Wasabi when someone leaves or a card goes missing.
  • Tells Wasabi what works and what doesn't, so it can be improved.

4Diners' and staff data

The restaurant is the controller of its diners' and staff data used in Wasabi (first names, orders, table activity, calls, bills, waiters' names); Wasabi processes it only to run Wasabi for the restaurant, under section 11 of Wasabi's Terms (version ), which is the data processing agreement for this pilot. In short: Wasabi never sells it or uses it for its own marketing; guests' first names are replaced with "Guest" after 30 days; data is stored with Google Firebase in Johannesburg, South Africa; and Wasabi tells the restaurant within 48 hours of any breach affecting its data.

6When the pilot ends

Either side can end the pilot at any time by telling the other by WhatsApp or email. When it ends, the restaurant chooses to subscribe, extend the pilot (agreed in writing), or stop. There is no automatic charge: nothing is paid unless the restaurant signs up in writing. The price after the pilot is a month or a year.

If the restaurant stops, its data is kept for 90 days so it can come back, a copy is given on request, and then it is deleted, as set out in section 4 of Wasabi's Terms.
