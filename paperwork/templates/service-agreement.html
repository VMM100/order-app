3. The main points of the Terms

  • Paid in advance each month or year, plus any taxes that apply. The owner page shows the date you're paid until.
  • Late payment: ordering keeps working for days after the paid-until date, then pauses until you pay. Nothing is deleted.
  • Price changes are announced at least 30 days ahead and apply from your next renewal.
  • Cancel any time by telling Wasabi; service runs to the end of the paid period, with no refund for part-used periods (Terms §4).

4. Data processing (Terms §11)

Wasabi processes the restaurant's diners' and staff data only to run Wasabi for the restaurant, keeps it confidential and secure, never sells it, and tells the restaurant within 48 hours of a breach affecting it. Guests' first names become "Guest" after 30 days; order records are kept 12 months.
